# 🧭 WorkCompass

**Your job search, organized.**

WorkCompass is a job search management platform designed to help people keep track of applications, interviews, follow-ups and opportunities — while also making the job search process easier to understand and manage.

## About

Searching for a job can quickly become difficult to manage: applications are spread across different platforms, interviews need to be remembered, follow-ups get forgotten, and comparing opportunities isn't always straightforward.

**WorkCompass brings the process together in one place.**

The idea is built around three simple steps:

### Track → Analyze → Decide

---

## ✨ Features

### 📋 Application Tracking

Keep job applications organized and follow their progress throughout the hiring process.

Track applications from initial submission through interviews, offers and final decisions.

### 📅 Interviews & Follow-ups

Use the calendar to keep track of upcoming interviews, deadlines and follow-ups.

### 📊 Job Search Insights

Get a clearer overview of how your job search is progressing through simple statistics and application insights.

### 🧭 Career Compass

Compare job opportunities based on factors that matter to you, including:

- Salary
- Work-life balance
- Remote flexibility
- Career growth
- Commute
- Company culture

Career Compass helps turn different priorities into a clearer comparison between opportunities.

### 🌿 Wellbeing Resources

Job searching can be stressful, so WorkCompass includes a dedicated wellbeing area with supportive resources.

General wellbeing resources are publicly accessible and **do not require an account**.

Signed-in users can also save personal wellbeing check-ins.

### 🌓 Light & Dark Mode

WorkCompass supports both light and dark themes, with the selected preference preserved across the application.

## 💡 Project Goal

WorkCompass was created to make job searching easier to organize, but the broader goal is to help users understand their search rather than simply collect applications.

Instead of focusing only on *where you applied*, WorkCompass aims to help answer:

- What opportunities are still active?
- What needs a follow-up?
- How is the job search progressing?
- Which opportunities fit your priorities best?

**Less chaos. Better decisions.**
